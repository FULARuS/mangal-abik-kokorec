Street food 101

What is kokoreç?

It's what Istanbul eats at 2 am. Lamb intestines, seasoned and wound around a long skewer, turn slowly over charcoal for hours. Then the usta chops it on a hot griddle with tomato, green pepper and spices and packs it into toasted bread. Not sure? Start with a half.

Slow over charcoalHours on the mangal, until it's crisp outside and juicy inside.
Chopped to orderDiced on the griddle with tomato, pepper, oregano and chili. Prefer it plain? Ask for İzmir style.
Pro tip: say “çıtır”Ask for your bread extra crispy. The regulars always do, and they know what they're talking about.
